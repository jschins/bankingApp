Home LAN

After you sign in on the remote laptop with the same Tailscale account / tailnet:
The device shows up automatically in the Tailscale admin console (and often in the Tailscale app’s machine list).
On the hub machine you don’t have to click anything special — if both are online on the same tailnet, they can reach each other.
Only if your tailnet has device approval / key expiry policies enabled, a new machine stays pending until you Approve it once in the admin console (not inside a special “server Tailscale” UI). Check Machines there if the remote laptop can’t ping 100.87.15.71.
